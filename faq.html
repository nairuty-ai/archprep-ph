Frequently asked questions

Updated Nov 2026

Do I need an account?

You can browse without one. To buy, open a reviewer or take any quiz (including free ones), log in with a 6-digit code sent to your email. No password. We don’t keep you signed in, so each visit needs a new code.

How do I pay?

GCash, Maya or QR Ph through PayMongo unlock your course instantly. You can also deposit to BDO, BPI, Metrobank, UnionBank or Landbank and upload your proof; we approve deposits within 24 hours.

How long do I have access?

Materials: until the next ALE date set by our admin. Quiz packs and the full mock board: 2 attempts each. After both are used, buy again for 2 more.

Can I download the reviewers?

No. Reviewers open in our online viewer, watermarked with your email. This keeps prices low by protecting the material from sharing.

Can I use more than one device?

One active session at a time. Closing the browser or 30 minutes idle ends it. If a session is stuck on another device, ask our admin to end it.

Which ALE subjects are covered?

Subjects A and B. Subject C (Architectural Design & Site Planning) is not offered yet.

How do referrals work?

Share your link. Every purchase made through it within 30 days adds ₱9 to your balance instantly. Once you reach ₱100 you can cash out to GCash. Coupons still work for your friends.

Is my name shown publicly?

Yes. Leaderboards are public and show your display name and best score. You can change your display name in Account.

Terms & refund policy

Effective Nov 1, 2026

All sales are final

Because content is available immediately after purchase, we do not offer refunds for change of mind, unused courses or exam results.

When we do refund

Failed payments where money was deducted, and duplicate charges for the same order. These are refunded to your original payment method (GCash, Maya, QR Ph source, or bank account for deposits).

How to request

Go to Account → Report a payment problem, choose the order and attach a screenshot. We review within 24 hours; refunds arrive in 3–5 banking days.

Account sharing

Accounts are personal. One active device per account. Sharing or redistributing material may lead to suspension without refund.

Referral rewards

Rewards for orders that are later refunded as duplicate or failed are voided. Self-referrals are not counted.

Privacy notice

Compliant with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173)

What we collect

Your email, display name, mobile/GCash number (only for referral cash-outs), purchases, quiz attempts and your active session.

Why

To log you in, deliver what you bought, grade quizzes, show leaderboards, pay referral rewards and prevent account sharing.

Public information

Your display name and best scores appear on public leaderboards.

Payments

Card and wallet details are handled by PayMongo; we never see or store them. Bank deposit proofs are stored privately and seen only by our admin.

Your rights
