Help & contact

Frequently asked questions

Everything about accounts, payments, quiz access, and referral rewards. Still stuck? Email us anytime.

Accounts & sign-in

Do I need an account to buy something?

Yes — you need a free account to purchase and access materials or quizzes. Creating one takes about 30 seconds: enter your email, enter the 6-digit code we send you, and you're in. No password, no card.

How do I sign in? Is there a password?

There is no password. We use a one-time email code every time you sign in. Enter your email on the sign-in page, enter the 6-digit code we send you, and you're logged in. Codes expire in 10 minutes.

I didn't receive the sign-in code. What do I do?

Check your spam or promotions folder first — the code comes from a no-reply address. If it's not there, go back to the sign-in page, re-enter your email, and tap Resend code. Codes expire after 10 minutes, so request a fresh one if yours is old.

Still nothing? Email us at rehinaneel@gmail.com and we'll help.

Payments

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept GCash and QR Ph only, through a secure HitPay payment page. We do not accept credit or debit cards at this time. We never see or store your payment details — everything goes through HitPay's secure checkout.

How quickly does my purchase appear in my account?

Instantly — the moment your GCash or QR Ph payment is confirmed by HitPay, the item unlocks in My Learning. No waiting, no emails, no codes to enter. If something doesn't appear within a few minutes of paying, contact us.

What's your refund policy?

Because materials and quizzes are digital and unlock instantly, sales are generally final. If you were charged incorrectly, or something genuinely went wrong with your order, email us at rehinaneel@gmail.com and we'll make it right.

Accessing your purchases

Where do I find my quizzes and materials after paying?

Everything is in My Learning. Log in, go to My Learning, and you'll see all your purchases. Quizzes have a Take quiz button; materials have an Open button that downloads or opens the file. No codes, no email links.

Can I use my account on my phone?

Yes. The whole site — including quizzes — is designed mobile-first and works smoothly on any phone or tablet. Review materials are PDF files you can open in any browser or PDF app.

How many times can I take a quiz?

Quiz attempts are set per pack — you'll see the attempt count in My Learning. Answers and explanations are shown after you submit, so each attempt is a fair test.

Referral program

How does the referral program work?

Sign in to get your personal referral link from My Account. Share the link with friends. When a friend uses your link and completes a purchase, you earn a ₱9 reward.

Once your available balance reaches ₱100, you can request a cash payout to your GCash number from the Earnings page.

When do I get paid for a referral?

Your referral reward appears as available after your friend's payment is confirmed. Payouts are processed manually — once you request one, we'll transfer the amount to your GCash and mark it as paid, usually within a few business days.

Still have a question?

We're happy to help. Email us and we'll get back to you.
