Help & contact

Frequently asked questions

Everything about delivery, payments, and quiz access codes. Still stuck? Email us anytime.

How do I receive my materials after paying?

After your payment is confirmed, we email your download link to the address you used to pay — usually within a few hours. Materials are shared manually, so please be patient and check your spam / promotions folder if you don't see it.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept GCash and QR Ph only, through a secure HitPay payment page. We do not accept credit or debit cards in this version, and we never see or store your payment details.

How do quiz access codes work?

When you buy a quiz pack, we email you an access code (for example ARCH-7F3K). Go to the quiz page, enter the quiz code (like structural-1) along with your access code, and your questions unlock.

Your score and full explanations appear immediately after you submit. Correct answers are never sent to your device until you submit, so the quiz stays a fair test.

Do codes expire?

Yes. Each code has an expiry date and may have a usage limit. If your code has expired or stopped working, email us and we'll sort it out.

What's your refund policy?

Because materials and quizzes are digital and delivered immediately on request, sales are generally final. If something went wrong with your order or you were charged incorrectly, contact us and we'll make it right.

Can I use my materials and quizzes on my phone?

Yes. The whole site, including the quizzes, is designed mobile-first and works smoothly on phones. Review materials are PDF/PowerPoint files you can open on any device.

I bought the wrong pack / didn't get my email. What do I do?

No problem — just email us at rehinaneel@gmail.com with the email address you paid with, and we'll help you out.

Still have a question?

We're happy to help. Email us and we'll get back to you.
