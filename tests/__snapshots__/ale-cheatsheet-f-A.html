Preservation vs restoration

SUBJ A

Preservation

  • Keeps what exists
  • Stops further decay
  • Minimal intervention

Restoration

  • Returns to a chosen period
  • May remove later additions
  • Needs strong evidence

Keep as is vs bring back as it was

Which document do you need?

SUBJ A
IF YOU NEED… → USE
  • Scope and fee in writing→Service agreement
  • Owner's requirements listed→Architectural program
  • A first design direction→Schematic design
  • A refined, coordinated design→Design development
  • Size, shape and location→Drawings
  • Quality of materials and work→Specifications
  • Binding construction terms→Contract documents

Waterproofing vs dampproofing

SUBJ B

Waterproofing

  • Resists water under pressure
  • Basements, roofs, wet areas
  • Membranes, coatings

Dampproofing

  • Resists moisture and vapour
  • Walls and slabs on grade
  • No hydrostatic pressure

Liquid water vs moisture

Easy to mix up

  • Average ≥ 70%≠Each subject ≥ 50%
  • 2 exam days≠3 graded subjects
  • Architect-of-record≠Architect-in-charge of construction
  • Sanitary drainage≠Storm drainage
  • PSO (ground coverage)≠FLAR (total floor area)
  • Amihan (NE, dry)≠Habagat (SW, rainy)
  • Master's credits 1 year≠Experience still required
